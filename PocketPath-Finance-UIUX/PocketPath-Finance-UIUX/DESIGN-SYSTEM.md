# PocketPath Design System

## Brand personality
Calm, optimistic, grounded, practical.

## Colors
- Forest: `#173D32` — navigation, headings, primary surfaces
- Lime: `#D6F06A` — primary actions and progress
- Warm background: `#F6F8F1`
- Soft sage: `#B8D2C0`
- Muted text: `#718078`
- Coral: `#D47750` — spending/attention
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#E5EBE3`

## Typography
Suggested font: Inter
- Page title: 39 px bold
- Section title: 20–22 px bold
- Card value: 30–36 px bold
- Body: 13–16 px
- Helper labels: 11–12 px

## Components to recreate
- Sidebar navigation item (default and active)
- Balance / income / spending summary cards
- Transaction row with category and amount
- Budget progress bar and circular progress ring
- Search field, filter controls, and primary button

## Shape and spacing
- Page inset: 42 px
- Card radius: 16–20 px
- Field/button radius: 10–12 px
- Common gaps: 16 px and 24 px
